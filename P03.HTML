Does TrollStore Work on Every iPhone?

No. TrollStore has specific firmware compatibility requirements.

Two iPhones of the same model may have different compatibility depending on their installed iOS versions.

Before looking for a TrollStore download, check the project's official documentation:
https://github.com/opa334/TrollStore

Do not rely on a third-party website that claims TrollStore works on every iPhone or every iOS version.

Does TrollStore Work on the Latest iOS Versions?

Not necessarily.

TrollStore relies on specific CoreTrust vulnerabilities in Apple's code-signing system. Compatibility is therefore limited to particular firmware versions.

A newer iOS release is not automatically supported just because an older version of the same iPhone model was compatible.

Always check the documented compatibility information before assuming that a download will work on your device.
