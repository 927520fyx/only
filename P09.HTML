Best TrollStore Alternatives in 2026: AltStore, SideStore, Sideloadly, and LiveContainer

Looking for the best TrollStore alternatives? If your iPhone or iPad cannot use TrollStore, or you need a different way to install and manage apps, several tools are worth considering.

However, not every alternative works like TrollStore. Some tools use Apple's regular app-signing process and require periodic refreshing. Others focus on managing apps from a computer or running multiple apps inside a shared environment.

The most useful way to compare these options is to look at what each tool actually replaces. Do you need an alternative installation method, fewer computer-dependent tasks, desktop-based IPA management, or a way to organize multiple apps?

This guide compares four commonly discussed options: AltStore, SideStore, Sideloadly, and LiveContainer. It explains their differences, practical limitations, and the situations in which each one may be worth researching.

What Should You Look for in a TrollStore Alternative?

Before comparing individual tools, identify the feature you actually need. The phrase "TrollStore alternative" can describe several different requirements.

1. App Installation Method

Some users simply want another way to install compatible IPA files. In that case, a conventional sideloading tool may be sufficient.

However, installing an IPA through a regular signing process is not the same as installing it through TrollStore. The resulting app may have different expiration and renewal requirements.

2. App Refreshing Requirements

One of the most important differences is whether installed apps need to be refreshed.

Traditional free Apple developer account sideloading generally has a limited signing period. Apps must be refreshed or signed again before that period expires. TrollStore's persistent installation behavior is different and is available only under its supported conditions.

If avoiding regular refreshing is your main priority, do not assume that another sideloading tool will provide the same experience.

3. Computer Dependence

Some tools rely on a desktop companion application. Others are designed to reduce the need for a computer after the initial setup.

This distinction matters if you prefer managing apps directly from your iPhone or iPad.

4. App Execution and Management

Not every related project is an installer.

For example, LiveContainer focuses on launching supported apps inside its own environment. That makes it relevant to some TrollStore-related searches, but it does not serve exactly the same purpose as a conventional IPA installation utility.

1. AltStore: A Traditional Sideloading Alternative

AltStore is one of the best-known options for users researching alternatives to TrollStore.

AltStore has two distinct versions: AltStore Classic and AltStore PAL. They have different distribution models and availability.

AltStore Classic is the version commonly associated with traditional sideloading. It uses AltServer, a companion application running on a computer, to install and refresh apps.

What Makes AltStore Different?

AltStore Classic provides an app interface for browsing and managing compatible apps. Users can also sideload supported IPA files.

Its conventional workflow includes:

  • Installing AltStore through AltServer.
  • Managing sideloaded apps through the AltStore interface.
  • Refreshing apps before their signing period expires.
  • Using the computer running AltServer for relevant installation and refresh tasks.

According to AltStore's documentation, apps installed with a regular free developer account expire after seven days. AltStore also documents Apple's limits on simultaneously active sideloaded apps and registered App IDs. These restrictions are important when evaluating whether the workflow suits your needs.

Advantages

  • Established sideloading ecosystem.
  • Dedicated interface for app management.
  • Supports compatible IPA files.
  • Provides documentation for installation and troubleshooting.

Limitations

  • The conventional Classic workflow depends on AltServer.
  • Free-account installations require periodic refreshing.
  • Apple's app and App ID restrictions still apply.

Who Should Consider AltStore?

AltStore Classic is worth considering if you want a familiar sideloading interface and are comfortable using a computer as part of the process.

It is not the right choice if your main requirement is reproducing TrollStore's persistent signing behavior.

AltStore PAL is a different distribution model, with regional availability and separate requirements. Do not assume its features and limitations are identical to AltStore Classic.

2. SideStore: An Option for On-Device Sideloading Management

SideStore is a community-driven project based on AltStore. Its main distinction is its goal of providing a more independent, on-device sideloading experience.

Unlike a workflow that depends on a computer for every routine refresh, SideStore is designed to reduce ongoing computer dependence after its initial installation.

How Is SideStore Different from AltStore?

SideStore uses an Apple Account and a personal development certificate to sign apps. It also uses an on-device VPN component to communicate with the required services.

Its documentation explains that a computer is needed for the initial installation. The LocalDevVPN component must be enabled when installing, updating, or refreshing apps.

This means SideStore is not completely computer-free. It is more accurate to describe it as a tool designed to reduce the need for a continuously connected computer.

Advantages

  • Designed for on-device app management.
  • Uses an Apple Account for signing.
  • Does not require a computer to remain continuously connected for normal use.
  • Offers an alternative workflow to conventional AltStore Classic management.

Limitations

  • Initial installation requires a computer.
  • The VPN component is part of its normal workflow.
  • Regular signing and refreshing restrictions still apply.
  • Setup and troubleshooting may require additional attention.

Who Should Consider SideStore?

SideStore may be worth researching if you prefer managing sideloaded apps on your iPhone or iPad rather than relying on a computer for routine tasks.

However, it does not remove Apple's regular signing limitations. If your main reason for searching for TrollStore alternatives is to avoid periodic app refreshing, SideStore does not provide the same behavior as TrollStore.

3. Sideloadly: A Desktop-Based IPA Installation Tool

Sideloadly takes a different approach. It is primarily a desktop utility for installing IPA files on supported Apple devices.

Rather than focusing on an on-device app marketplace experience, Sideloadly provides a computer-based interface for managing installations.

Main Features

Sideloadly's official website documents features including:

  • IPA installation.
  • Windows and macOS support.
  • Apple Account integration.
  • Wi-Fi sideloading after device pairing.
  • Automatic app refreshing through its background daemon.

The automatic refresh feature still depends on the computer being available through a supported USB or Wi-Fi connection. It should not be confused with permanent signing.

Advantages

  • Useful for users who prefer desktop-based management.
  • Supports IPA installation.
  • Offers both USB and Wi-Fi workflows.
  • Includes an automatic refreshing feature.

Limitations

  • Its main workflow is computer-based.
  • Free-account signing restrictions still apply.
  • Automatic refreshing depends on the computer and connection being available.
  • It does not reproduce TrollStore's persistent installation model.

Who Should Consider Sideloadly?

Sideloadly is worth considering if you prefer managing IPA installations from Windows or macOS.

It may be a more suitable option than an on-device manager if you want to handle installation tasks from a desktop environment. However, it is not designed to eliminate the signing and expiration restrictions associated with conventional sideloading.

4. LiveContainer: An Alternative Way to Run and Organize Apps

LiveContainer is different from the other tools in this comparison.

It describes itself as an app launcher that runs supported iOS applications inside its own environment rather than installing every app as a separate conventional iOS application.

This makes it relevant to users researching alternative app management approaches, but it is not a direct replacement for every TrollStore feature.

What Makes LiveContainer Different?

According to its project documentation, LiveContainer supports features such as:

  • Running supported apps inside its environment.
  • Managing multiple app containers.
  • Keeping multiple versions of an app in separate data containers.
  • Multitasking on supported configurations.

The project also documents requirements and limitations that users should review before relying on it.

For example, not every app is compatible. App extensions and some system-level behaviors have limitations, and guest app containers are not fully sandboxed from one another.

Advantages

  • Offers a different approach to app organization.
  • Supports multiple containers for compatible apps.
  • Can manage multiple versions of an app.
  • Provides multitasking features on supported configurations.

Limitations

  • It is not a conventional replacement for an IPA installer.
  • Not every app works inside its environment.
  • Some app permissions and extensions have limitations.
  • Its behavior depends on its installation and signing configuration.

Who Should Consider LiveContainer?

LiveContainer may be interesting if your priority is running and organizing multiple supported apps within a shared environment.

If you simply want a tool that installs apps in the same way as TrollStore, it may not match your requirements. Its purpose is different, so compare its features with your actual use case before choosing it.

TrollStore vs. Its Alternatives: What Is the Real Difference?

The most important distinction is not which tool has the longest feature list. It is whether the tool provides the specific behavior you expect.

TrollStore vs. AltStore

TrollStore provides persistent installation on specifically supported firmware. AltStore Classic uses Apple's regular developer signing process.

Choose AltStore Classic when you want a conventional sideloading workflow. TrollStore remains different in its signing behavior, but only works under its supported conditions.

TrollStore vs. SideStore

SideStore focuses on on-device sideloading management and reducing ongoing computer dependence.

It still uses Apple's developer signing system and requires periodic refreshing. It should not be treated as a way to reproduce TrollStore's persistent installation behavior.

TrollStore vs. Sideloadly

Sideloadly is primarily a desktop IPA installation utility. It is useful for people who prefer managing installations from a computer.

TrollStore and Sideloadly have different signing models, so they solve different problems.

TrollStore vs. LiveContainer

TrollStore is an application installation utility. LiveContainer is an application launcher that runs supported apps inside its own environment.

This is a comparison between two different approaches to app management, not a direct feature-for-feature replacement.

A Practical Way to Make Your Decision

Before choosing a tool, consider these questions:

  • Do you mainly want to install IPA files? AltStore Classic and Sideloadly are more directly relevant to conventional IPA sideloading.
  • Do you prefer managing apps on your iPhone or iPad? SideStore is designed around on-device management, although its initial installation and VPN requirements should be considered.
  • Do you want to organize multiple apps in one environment? LiveContainer may be more relevant than a traditional sideloading utility.
  • Are you trying to avoid app expiration? Be careful with this requirement. The alternatives discussed here do not automatically provide TrollStore's persistent signing behavior. Conventional signing workflows have their own renewal restrictions.

Is TrollStore Lite a General TrollStore Alternative?

TrollStore Lite is worth mentioning because its name can make it appear to be a general alternative for devices that cannot use standard TrollStore.

That interpretation is misleading.

The official TrollStore release notes describe TrollStore Lite in connection with jailbroken iOS versions. It is a related variant with a different use case, not a general solution that makes unsupported firmware compatible with standard TrollStore.

For that reason, it should not be placed in the same category as AltStore, SideStore, or Sideloadly when comparing conventional alternatives.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best alternative to TrollStore?

There is no universal winner. AltStore Classic, SideStore, and Sideloadly are conventional sideloading options with different workflows. LiveContainer serves a different purpose by running supported apps inside its own environment.

Which TrollStore alternative does not require a computer?

SideStore is designed to reduce ongoing computer dependence after initial installation. However, its official documentation still requires a computer for the initial setup.

Is SideStore better than AltStore?

It depends on your preferred workflow. SideStore focuses on on-device management, while AltStore Classic uses AltServer as a computer companion. Both remain subject to regular signing restrictions.

Does Sideloadly work like TrollStore?

No. Sideloadly uses a conventional signing and sideloading approach. It does not provide the same persistent installation behavior as TrollStore.

Is LiveContainer an app installer?

LiveContainer is primarily an app launcher. It runs supported applications inside its own environment rather than functioning as a direct equivalent to a conventional IPA installer.

Can these alternatives install every IPA file?

No. App compatibility depends on the app itself, its system requirements, signing requirements, and the capabilities of the selected tool. An IPA file that works with one installation method may not work with another.

Does AltStore require an Apple Account?

AltStore Classic uses an Apple Account as part of its conventional sideloading workflow. Its signing limits and app restrictions depend on the account type and Apple's current policies.

Is TrollStore Lite suitable for unsupported iOS versions?

Do not assume so. TrollStore Lite is associated with selected jailbroken environments and should not be treated as a general compatibility solution for unsupported firmware.

Final Thoughts: Choosing the Right TrollStore Alternative

The best TrollStore alternative depends on what you actually need.

AltStore Classic is worth considering for conventional sideloading and app management. SideStore is designed for a more independent, on-device workflow. Sideloadly is suited to users who prefer installing IPA files from a computer. LiveContainer offers a different approach by running supported apps inside a shared environment.

The key point: These tools are not interchangeable. In particular, conventional sideloading should not be confused with TrollStore's persistent installation behavior.

Before choosing one, review its official documentation, current compatibility information, signing requirements, and known limitations. That will help you select a tool based on its actual capabilities rather than its name or popularity.
