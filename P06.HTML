TrollStore Supported Devices: Which iPhone and iPad Models Are Compatible?

TrollStore is an iOS application installer that allows users to install compatible IPA applications with persistent signing. However, it does not work on every iPhone or iPad.

Device compatibility depends on more than the model name. The installed iOS or iPadOS version, processor architecture, and availability of a compatible installation method all affect whether TrollStore can be used.

For example, an iPhone 15 may have newer hardware than an iPhone 8, but that does not automatically mean it has better TrollStore compatibility.

This guide explains which iPhone and iPad models are worth checking, how processor generations affect compatibility, and why hardware compatibility must be considered separately from firmware support.

How TrollStore Device Compatibility Works

TrollStore compatibility involves three main factors:

  • Device model: Determines the processor generation and hardware capabilities.
  • Processor architecture: Different hardware generations may have different installation-method requirements.
  • iOS or iPadOS version: TrollStore relies on specific CoreTrust vulnerabilities that are present only in certain firmware releases.

A device must meet the relevant hardware and software requirements. Even when its processor is compatible, an unsupported operating system can prevent TrollStore from working.

It is therefore important to distinguish between a device that can run a supported version of iOS and a device that is confirmed to have a compatible installation method.

Which iPhone Models Support TrollStore?

Several iPhone generations are commonly associated with TrollStore compatibility.

However, the following models should be treated as devices to evaluate rather than a guarantee that every firmware configuration is supported.

iPhone 5s

The iPhone 5s uses Apple's A7 processor.

Its maximum supported iOS version is below TrollStore's documented minimum firmware requirement.

Consequently, the iPhone 5s should not be considered compatible with the established TrollStore releases.

iPhone 6s and iPhone 7 Series

The iPhone 6s, iPhone 6s Plus, iPhone 7, and iPhone 7 Plus belong to older hardware generations.

These devices use the A9 and A10 Fusion processors, respectively.

Unlike the iPhone 5s, they support iOS versions within TrollStore's documented compatibility range.

However, the installed firmware and the requirements of the available installation method must still be considered.

The processor generation alone does not establish whether a particular device configuration is supported.

iPhone 8 and iPhone X

The iPhone 8, iPhone 8 Plus, and iPhone X use Apple's A11 Bionic processor.

These models belong to the same general hardware generation, although their individual firmware histories may differ.

When evaluating TrollStore compatibility, check the exact iOS version and the installation-method requirements rather than assuming that all A11 devices have identical compatibility.

iPhone XR and iPhone XS Series

The iPhone XR, iPhone XS, and iPhone XS Max use Apple's A12 Bionic processor.

They represent a newer hardware generation than the iPhone X.

Their processor architecture is relevant when comparing installation-method requirements with older devices.

However, having a newer processor does not remove TrollStore's firmware limitations.

A supported iOS version remains essential.

iPhone 11 and Newer Models

This category includes:

  • iPhone 11 series
  • iPhone 12 series
  • iPhone 13 series
  • iPhone 14 series
  • iPhone 15 series

These devices use newer Apple processors and belong to later hardware generations.

Nevertheless, newer hardware does not automatically provide TrollStore compatibility.

For example, a newer iPhone running an unsupported iOS release cannot become compatible simply because its processor is more powerful.

The exact firmware version and an appropriate installation method must still be verified.

Which iPad Models Support TrollStore?

TrollStore compatibility is not limited to iPhones. Certain iPad models can also meet the necessary hardware and firmware requirements.

However, different iPad generations use different processors and support different iPadOS releases.

iPad mini Models

The iPad mini family includes several generations worth evaluating:

  • iPad mini 4
  • iPad mini 5
  • iPad mini 6

These models have different processor generations and software support histories.

For this reason, users should identify the exact iPad mini generation rather than treating the entire product family as one compatibility category.

Standard iPad Models

Standard iPad models have also used several different processor generations.

Examples include:

  • iPad 6th generation
  • iPad 7th generation
  • iPad 8th generation
  • iPad 9th generation

The iPad 6 uses the A10 Fusion processor, while the iPad 9 uses the A13 Bionic.

This hardware difference is relevant when evaluating device-specific requirements.

However, the installed iPadOS version remains an essential part of the compatibility assessment.

iPad Air Models

Different iPad Air generations use different Apple processors.

Older models and newer models may therefore have different hardware characteristics and installation-method requirements.

Do not assume that compatibility information for one iPad Air generation applies to every other generation.

iPad Pro Models

The iPad Pro family includes multiple hardware generations and processor configurations.

Some models use A-series processors, while newer generations include Apple silicon from the M series.

Because of these differences, an exact model identification is more useful than simply knowing that a device belongs to the iPad Pro family.

Does Processor Architecture Affect TrollStore Compatibility?

Processor architecture is another factor worth understanding when researching TrollStore supported devices.

Two common architecture categories are:

  • arm64: Used by older 64-bit Apple devices.
  • arm64e: Used by newer Apple processor generations.

The iPhone 6s through iPhone X belong to the older arm64 hardware group, while the iPhone XS and newer generations generally belong to the arm64e group.

These differences can affect which installation methods are available for a particular device and firmware combination.

However, processor architecture should not be confused with firmware compatibility.

A device using a supported architecture may still run an iOS version outside TrollStore's documented compatibility range.

Why Device Compatibility Does Not Guarantee Support

One of the most common misunderstandings about TrollStore is that a compatible iPhone model automatically means TrollStore will work.

This is not correct.

TrollStore depends on specific vulnerabilities in Apple's CoreTrust code-signing system.

Apple addressed the vulnerabilities used by established TrollStore releases in later firmware versions.

The documented compatibility range includes:

  • iOS/iPadOS 14.0 beta 2 through 16.6.1
  • iOS/iPadOS 16.7 RC, Build 20H18
  • iOS/iPadOS 17.0

Other firmware releases, including iOS 16.7 final and iOS 17.0.1 or later, are outside this documented compatibility range.

This means that two identical iPhone models can have different TrollStore compatibility depending on their installed firmware.

How to Identify Your iPhone or iPad Model

Before evaluating TrollStore compatibility, identify the exact device model and installed operating system.

You can find these details in your device settings:

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Select General.
  3. Tap About.
  4. Check the Model Name.
  5. Find the Software Version.

Record both the device model and software version.

These two details provide a more reliable starting point than searching using only a general product name such as iPhone 13 or iPad Pro.

For certain firmware releases, the exact build number may also be relevant.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Does TrollStore work on every iPhone?

No. TrollStore only works with compatible device and firmware configurations. The iPhone model alone is not enough to determine compatibility.

2. Does TrollStore support iPhone 6s?

The iPhone 6s supports firmware generations within TrollStore's documented compatibility range. However, the installed iOS version and available installation method must still be checked.

3. Can I use TrollStore on iPhone 7?

The iPhone 7 uses the A10 Fusion processor and can run supported iOS generations. Actual compatibility depends on its firmware and installation-method requirements.

4. Does TrollStore support iPhone XR?

The iPhone XR uses the A12 Bionic processor. Its hardware generation can be evaluated for TrollStore compatibility, but the installed iOS version remains essential.

5. Does TrollStore work on iPhone 15?

The iPhone 15 series has newer hardware, but this does not guarantee compatibility with every iOS version. The documented firmware requirements still apply.

6. Can TrollStore be used on an iPad?

Yes, certain iPad models can meet TrollStore's hardware and firmware requirements. The exact model and installed iPadOS version must be evaluated together.

7. Is processor architecture more important than the iPhone model?

Neither factor should be considered independently. The model identifies the hardware generation, while processor architecture and firmware help determine which compatibility requirements apply.

8. Does updating iOS affect TrollStore compatibility?

Yes. Updating to a firmware version outside the documented compatibility range can make TrollStore unavailable, even when the device hardware itself is compatible.

9. Is a newer iPhone always better for TrollStore?

No. A newer iPhone may have more advanced hardware but run an unsupported iOS version. Firmware compatibility is a separate requirement.

10. Where can I find reliable TrollStore compatibility information?

The original TrollStore project repository and established installation documentation are the appropriate references for checking compatibility information.

Final Verdict: Is Your Device Compatible With TrollStore?

Determining whether an iPhone or iPad supports TrollStore requires more than checking its model name.

The most important factors are:

  • Exact device model and hardware generation.
  • Processor architecture.
  • Installed iOS or iPadOS version.
  • Availability of a compatible installation method.

Older devices are not automatically compatible, and newer devices are not automatically excluded.

The best approach is to evaluate the complete device and firmware combination rather than relying on a general list of supported iPhone and iPad models.

Always check the original TrollStore documentation and current compatibility references before relying on a compatibility claim.
