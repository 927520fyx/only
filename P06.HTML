TrollStore Supported Devices: iPhone and iPad Models by Chip Architecture

TrollStore compatibility is closely related to the device’s chip architecture. Different iPhone and iPad models use different processors, which fall into two main groups: arm64 (A8–A11) and arm64e (A12 and newer).

This page focuses only on device models and their chip architecture. It helps you quickly identify which group your iPhone or iPad belongs to.

1. Two Main Architecture Groups

Apple devices used with TrollStore are commonly divided into two architecture groups based on their processors:

Architecture Chip Range Typical Release Period
arm64 A8 – A11 2014 – 2017
arm64e A12 and newer 2018 and later

These two groups often have different installation method availability. Knowing which group your device belongs to is the first step when checking TrollStore-related information.

2. iPhone Models by Architecture

arm64 (A8 – A11) iPhones

These models use older Apple processors and belong to the arm64 group:

  • iPhone 6 / 6 Plus (A8)
  • iPhone 6s / 6s Plus (A9)
  • iPhone SE (1st generation) (A9)
  • iPhone 7 / 7 Plus (A10)
  • iPhone 8 / 8 Plus (A11)
  • iPhone X (A11)

arm64e (A12 and newer) iPhones

These models use newer processors and belong to the arm64e group:

  • iPhone XR / XS / XS Max (A12)
  • iPhone 11 / 11 Pro / 11 Pro Max (A13)
  • iPhone SE (2nd generation) (A13)
  • iPhone 12 series (A14)
  • iPhone 13 series (A15)
  • iPhone SE (3rd generation) (A15)
  • iPhone 14 series (A15 / A16)
  • iPhone 15 series (A16 / A17 Pro)
  • iPhone 16 series (A18 / A18 Pro)

Note: iPhone 5s and earlier models use even older chips (A7 and below) and fall outside the commonly discussed TrollStore device range.

3. iPad Models by Architecture

arm64 (A8 – A11) iPads

  • iPad mini 4 (A8)
  • iPad Air 2 (A8X)
  • iPad (5th generation) (A9)
  • iPad (6th generation) (A10)
  • iPad (7th generation) (A10)

arm64e (A12 and newer) iPads

  • iPad mini 5 (A12)
  • iPad mini 6 (A15)
  • iPad Air (3rd generation and newer)
  • iPad (8th generation and newer)
  • iPad Pro (2018 and later, including M1/M2/M4 models)

4. How to Check Your Device Group

To find out which architecture group your iPhone or iPad belongs to:

  1. Open the Settings app.
  2. Go to General → About.
  3. Look at the Model Name.
  4. Match the model with the lists above.

As a simple rule of thumb:

  • Most devices released in 2017 or earlier → arm64 group
  • Most devices released in 2018 or later → arm64e group

5. Frequently Asked Questions

Which iPhones are arm64?

The main arm64 iPhones still commonly discussed are iPhone 6s, iPhone 7, iPhone 8, and iPhone X series.

Which iPhones are arm64e?

iPhone XR and all iPhone models released after it (11, 12, 13, 14, 15, and 16 series) use arm64e architecture.

Does TrollStore work on iPhone 8?

iPhone 8 and iPhone 8 Plus belong to the arm64 (A11) group. Whether TrollStore can be used also depends on the installed system version and available installation methods.

Does TrollStore work on iPhone X?

iPhone X also uses the A11 chip and belongs to the same arm64 group as the iPhone 8 series.

Does TrollStore work on iPhone 11 / 12 / 13 / 14 / 15?

These models all belong to the arm64e group. Actual usability depends on the system version currently installed on the device.

Does the chip architecture alone decide if TrollStore works?

No. Architecture only tells you which group the device belongs to. Whether TrollStore can actually be installed also depends on the system version and the installation methods available for that combination.

Where can I check supported system versions?

For detailed iOS and iPadOS version compatibility, please refer to the dedicated TrollStore iOS Compatibility page on this site.
