TrollStore Supported Devices: Compatible iPhone and iPad Models

Not every iPhone or iPad can run TrollStore. Device compatibility depends on the processor architecture, installed iOS or iPadOS version, and the availability of a compatible installation method.

An older iPhone may support TrollStore while a newer model running a later firmware version may not. This is because TrollStore relies on specific vulnerabilities in Apple's CoreTrust code-signing system rather than simply depending on processor performance or device age.

This guide provides a detailed overview of TrollStore supported devices, including iPhone and iPad models, processor generations, architecture classifications, firmware compatibility, and installation limitations.

If you want to determine whether your device can use TrollStore, start by identifying its exact model and installed operating system version.

Which Devices Are Compatible With TrollStore?

TrollStore supports a range of 64-bit Apple devices, including selected iPhone and iPad generations.

However, three conditions must be considered when evaluating compatibility:

  • Hardware compatibility: The device must belong to a supported hardware and processor category.
  • Firmware compatibility: The installed iOS or iPadOS version must fall within TrollStore's supported range.
  • Installation compatibility: An installation method must be available for the specific combination of hardware and firmware.

These conditions are related but are not interchangeable.

For example, an iPhone 15 has modern hardware, but an iPhone 15 running iOS 17.0.1 cannot use the standard TrollStore CoreTrust compatibility that applies to iOS 17.0.

Similarly, an older iPhone may have compatible hardware but still be excluded because its maximum supported operating system version is below TrollStore's minimum requirement.

Which iOS and iPadOS Versions Support TrollStore?

TrollStore relies on specific CoreTrust vulnerabilities. As a result, its firmware compatibility is limited to particular releases.

The documented compatibility range is:

iOS / iPadOS version Compatibility
14.0 beta 1 and earlier Not supported
14.0 beta 2–14.8.1 Supported range
15.0–15.8.8 Supported ranges with version-specific installation methods
16.0–16.6.1 Supported
16.7 RC (Build 20H18) Special supported release
16.7 public release and other 16.7.x releases Not supported
17.0 beta 1–final release Supported
17.0.1 and later Not supported

Some later maintenance releases within the iOS 15 family require special installation methods, including TrollRestore.

This means that the general firmware range should not be interpreted as a single universal installation procedure.

The distinction between iOS 16.7 RC and the public iOS 16.7 release is especially important. Only the specified RC build is included in the documented compatibility range.

Likewise, iOS 17.0 must not be confused with iOS 17.0.1.

For a more detailed explanation of supported operating system releases, see our TrollStore iOS Compatibility Guide.

How Does Processor Architecture Affect TrollStore Compatibility?

Processor architecture is one of the factors used to determine which TrollStore installation method applies to a device.

Two major categories are relevant.

arm64 Devices

The arm64 category includes older Apple processors such as:

  • A8 and A8X
  • A9 and A9X
  • A10 and A10X
  • A11

Examples include the iPhone 6s, iPhone 7, iPhone 8, and iPhone X.

The installation compatibility matrix separates A8 devices from A9–A11 devices because their installation requirements are not identical.

arm64e Devices

The arm64e category includes newer Apple processor generations, including:

  • A12 and A12X
  • A13
  • A14
  • A15
  • A16
  • A17 Pro
  • M1 and M2

Examples include the iPhone XR, iPhone 11, iPhone 12, iPhone 13, iPhone 14, iPhone 15, and several iPad Pro generations.

Although these devices share the arm64e classification, their installation compatibility still depends on the exact firmware version.

Architecture is therefore a useful classification tool, not an independent guarantee of TrollStore support.

Why Does a Newer iPhone Not Always Support TrollStore?

A common misconception is that newer iPhones automatically have better TrollStore compatibility.

In reality, TrollStore's compatibility is determined by specific vulnerabilities in Apple's code-signing system.

When Apple patches those vulnerabilities in later operating system releases, devices running those releases fall outside the documented compatibility range.

Consider two examples:

  • Example 1: iPhone 7 — An iPhone 7 can run supported iOS versions within its firmware history. Its A10 processor belongs to the arm64 installation category.
  • Example 2: iPhone 15 — An iPhone 15 has substantially newer hardware and uses an arm64e processor. However, its compatibility depends on the exact iOS version. iOS 17.0 is within TrollStore's supported range, while iOS 17.0.1 and later are not.

These examples demonstrate why device generation alone cannot establish compatibility.

How to Check Whether Your iPhone or iPad Is Compatible

You can identify the information needed for a compatibility assessment directly from your device settings.

Follow these steps:

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Select General.
  3. Tap About.
  4. Find the Model Name.
  5. Check the Software Version.
  6. If necessary, identify the exact build number.

After collecting this information, compare your device against the hardware and firmware compatibility tables in this guide.

The assessment should follow this order:

  • Step 1: Identify the hardware — Find the exact iPhone or iPad model and its processor generation.
  • Step 2: Identify the firmware — Check the complete iOS or iPadOS version rather than relying only on the major version number.
  • Step 3: Check the architecture category — Determine whether the device belongs to the A8, A9–A11, or A12–A17/M1–M2 category used by the installation compatibility matrix.
  • Step 4: Verify the installation requirements — Check whether a documented installation method exists for the exact firmware and hardware combination.

A device should only be classified as compatible when these conditions have been established.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does TrollStore Work on All iPhone Models?

No. TrollStore does not work on every iPhone model or firmware version.

The device must meet the hardware and firmware requirements, and a compatible installation method must be available.

Does TrollStore Support iPhone 6s?

The iPhone 6s uses the A9 processor and belongs to the arm64 category.

It has firmware versions within TrollStore's documented compatibility range. However, the exact iOS release and installation requirements must be checked.

Does TrollStore Work on iPhone 15?

The iPhone 15 family uses A16 or A17 Pro processors, depending on the model.

Its hardware belongs to the arm64e category. However, TrollStore compatibility depends on the installed firmware.

iOS 17.0 is supported, whereas iOS 17.0.1 and later are outside the documented compatibility range.

Which iPad Models Support TrollStore?

Supported hardware generations include the iPad mini 4 and later, iPad 5th generation and later, iPad Air 2 and later, and various iPad Pro models.

The exact iPadOS version and installation method must still be verified.

What Is the Difference Between arm64 and arm64e?

arm64 and arm64e are different Apple processor architecture categories.

Older devices, including the iPhone 6s through iPhone X, belong to the arm64 category. The iPhone XS and newer A12-based generations belong to the arm64e category.

These categories help determine which installation methods are applicable.

Is iOS 17.0.1 Compatible With TrollStore?

No. The documented CoreTrust compatibility range includes iOS 17.0 but excludes iOS 17.0.1 and later releases.

The distinction is important because even a device with compatible hardware cannot overcome an unsupported firmware version.

Final Verdict: Which Devices Can Run TrollStore?

TrollStore compatibility is not determined by an iPhone or iPad model alone.

The processor architecture, operating system version, and availability of an appropriate installation method must all be considered.

Older devices such as the iPhone 6s, iPhone 7, and iPhone 8 can have compatible firmware configurations, while newer devices such as the iPhone 15 may also qualify when running the specific supported firmware release.

On the other hand, some devices are excluded because their supported operating system history does not overlap with TrollStore's requirements.

Before making a compatibility decision, always verify the exact device model, firmware version, build number, and applicable installation method.

For the most reliable information, consult the original TrollStore project and its maintained installation compatibility documentation.
