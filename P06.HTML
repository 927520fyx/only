TrollStore Supported Devices Matrix: iPhone, iPad & iPod touch Compatibility

Whether an iPhone, iPad, or iPod touch can install TrollStore depends on two hardware boundaries: the device's factory-shipped iOS/iPadOS version (which sets the earliest firmware it can ever run) and its processor architecture (arm64 vs. arm64e, which determines which installer utility works).

Devices powered by chips from the A8 through A17 Pro and M1 through M2 have at least one firmware build inside TrollStore's supported window (14.0 beta 2 – 16.6.1, 16.7 RC 20H18, and 17.0). Older A7 devices cap out too early (iOS 12.5.7), while M4 iPads and iPhone 16 models shipped after Apple patched the CoreTrust bug.

Use the hardware matrices below to look up your exact model, verify its eligible firmware window, and see which installation tool applies to your chip generation.

How Chip Architecture Dictates Installation Tools

Even when two devices run the exact same iOS version, their processor group determines which exploit chain and installer utility can inject the Persistence Helper:

1. A8 / A8X Devices (arm64 with 4K Page Size)

The iPad mini 4 and iPad Air 2 are the only TrollStore-compatible devices built on the A8 architecture. Because A8 chips use a 4K kernel page size (while A9 and newer use 16K pages), some kernel-exploit-based installers behave differently on them. On iPadOS 15.x, these tablets typically rely on TrollMisaka, TrollInstallerX (supported builds), or TrollRestore.

2. A9 – A11 Devices (arm64 without PAC)

Covering the iPhone 6s through iPhone X, iPod touch 7, and iPad 5–7 / Pro 1st–2nd gen, these chips lack Pointer Authentication Codes (PAC). TrollInstallerX supports them cleanly from iOS 14.0 beta 2 up to 16.6.1 (and 15.8.3 on A9/A10). Additionally, because A9–A11 hardware is vulnerable to the bootrom-level checkm8 exploit, users on unsupported versions like iOS 16.7.x (non-RC) can still jailbreak with palera1n and use TrollStore Lite (which requires a jailbroken state to launch apps).

3. A12 – A17 Pro & M1 – M2 Devices (arm64e with PAC)

Starting with the iPhone XR/XS and iPad mini 5 / Air 3 / Pro 2018, Apple introduced arm64e with hardware Pointer Authentication (PAC). On these devices, direct kernel-exploit installation via TrollInstallerX tops out at iOS 16.6.1. For iOS 16.7 RC (20H18) and iOS 17.0, there is no on-device kernel exploit for arm64e—installation requires TrollRestore over USB from a Windows, macOS, or Linux computer by modifying a device backup to replace a system app with the Persistence Helper.

Hardware With Zero Compatible Firmware

Some Apple devices can never run standard TrollStore regardless of what software you download, because their entire supported iOS/iPadOS lifecycle falls either before or after the CoreTrust vulnerability window:

  • iPhone 5s, iPhone 6, iPhone 6 Plus, iPad Air (1st gen), iPad mini 2 & 3 (A7 / A8): Apple ended software updates for these models at iOS 12.5.7. Because TrollStore's CoreTrust bug was introduced in iOS 14.0 beta 2, these devices cannot reach the minimum firmware threshold.
  • iPad Pro (M4) & iPad Air (M2, 2024): Shipped out of the box with iPadOS 17.5 (May 2024), long after Apple patched the CoreTrust flaw in 17.0.1.
  • iPhone 16, 16 Plus, 16 Pro, 16 Pro Max & iPad mini (A17 Pro): Shipped out of the box with iOS 18.0 / iPadOS 18.0 (late 2024). No compatible firmware build exists for these models.

Beware of Fake "iOS 18 / iPhone 16" Installers: Any website or YouTube video claiming to offer a TrollStore IPA or profile installer for iPhone 16 or M4 iPads is distributing configuration profiles (web clips) or adware, not opa334's TrollStore.

4-Step Device & Build Verification

Before downloading an installer binary, check these four fields on your device under Settings > General > About:

  1. Confirm Model Name: Match your device against the iPhone or iPad matrix above to identify whether it is arm64 (A8–A11) or arm64e (A12–A17 Pro / M1–M2).
  2. Tap "iOS Version" to Reveal the Build Number: Tapping the version string exposes the parenthetical build code. If your version reads 16.7, verify the build is exactly 20H18 (16.7 RC). Public 16.7 builds (such as 20H19) are patched.
  3. Match Version to Installer Tool: Use TrollInstallerX for 14.0b2–16.6.1 (or TrollMisaka on A8), and use TrollRestore (PC/Mac required) for 16.7 RC (20H18), 17.0, or late 15.8.4+ maintenance builds.
  4. Verify Official Releases Only: Obtain TrollStore and installer binaries exclusively from github.com/opa334/TrollStore and the iOS CFW Guide.

Device-Specific Questions (FAQ)

Does iPod touch 7th Generation Support TrollStore?

Yes. The iPod touch (7th generation) uses the A10 Fusion (arm64) chip and supports iOS 12.3 through iOS 15.8.x. Any unit running iOS 14.0 beta 2 through 15.8.x is eligible for TrollStore via TrollInstallerX or TrollRestore.

Why Do iPhone 13 and iPhone 14 Have Different Compatibility Windows Despite Both Using the A15 Chip?

Because of their launch dates. The iPhone 13 launched in September 2021 with iOS 15.0, giving it compatibility across iOS 15.0 – 17.0. The iPhone 14 and 14 Plus launched in September 2022 with iOS 16.0 preinstalled—they cannot be downgraded to iOS 15, limiting their eligible window to iOS 16.0 – 17.0.

How Can I Tell If a Sealed or Used iPhone 15 Still Has Compatible iOS 17.0?

Only the very first production batch of iPhone 15 / 15 Pro units manufactured in September 2023 shipped with iOS 17.0. Apple pushed iOS 17.0.1 and 17.0.2 during launch week, which fixed the CoreTrust bug. Unless a seller can show a screenshot of Settings > General > About displaying 17.0, assume any sealed or used iPhone 15 is on 17.0.1+ and incompatible.

Why Are A8 Devices Treated Differently From A9–A11 Devices?

While A8 (iPad mini 4, iPad Air 2) and A9–A11 devices are all arm64, A8 processors use a 4K kernel page size instead of the 16K page size introduced on the A9. Kernel exploits built specifically for 16K page sizes do not work out of the box on A8 tablets, which is why compatibility matrices list A8 in its own row.

Can M4 iPad Pro or iPhone 16 Models Run TrollStore?

No. The M4 iPad Pro shipped with iPadOS 17.5 and the iPhone 16 series shipped with iOS 18.0. Because both devices launched long after Apple patched the CoreTrust vulnerability in iOS 17.0.1, no compatible firmware exists for them.
